# Game-Gauntlet-2026-jam


> **One intersection. Seven days. Increasing chaos. Don't get fired.**

**[Game Name]** is a **2D top-down traffic management game** where you play as a newly recruited traffic officer assigned to one of the busiest intersections in the city.

Your mission sounds simple: **keep the traffic moving.**

Unfortunately, the city has other plans.

Over the course of **7 increasingly difficult days**, you'll control traffic lights, manage pedestrians, clear paths for emergency vehicles, and try to prevent the intersection from descending into complete chaos.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

You are responsible for controlling a busy city intersection.

You'll need to:

* 🚦 Control traffic lights
* 🚗 Keep vehicles moving efficiently
* 🚶 Prevent pedestrians from waiting too long
* 🚑 Give ambulances and emergency vehicles priority
* 💥 Avoid collisions and accidents
* 🚌 Deal with different types of vehicles
* ⏱️ Keep average waiting times under control
* 📊 Maintain a good performance rating

There isn't always one correct traffic-light pattern.

You'll need to **watch what's happening and react accordingly.**

---

## 📅 Survive 7 Days

The game takes place across **7 days**, with each day becoming progressively harder.

### 🌅 Morning

Traffic starts relatively calm, giving you time to understand the intersection and prepare for what's coming.

### ☀️ Afternoon

More cars begin entering the intersection and waiting times start increasing.

### 🌆 Evening Rush

Traffic density rises dramatically.

More cars.

More pedestrians.

More impatient drivers.

More emergency vehicles.

More problems.

By **Day 7**, the peaceful intersection from Day 1 should feel like complete controlled chaos.

---

## 📈 Dynamic Difficulty

Difficulty increases in two ways:

**Day-by-day progression**

Each new day introduces heavier traffic and more difficult situations.

**Traffic escalation during the day**

Even within a single day, traffic gradually becomes more intense.

```text
Morning
   ↓
Light Traffic
   ↓
Moderate Traffic
   ↓
Heavy Traffic
   ↓
Evening Rush Hour
   ↓
ABSOLUTE CHAOS
```

---

## 🚗 Vehicle Types

Different vehicles behave differently, forcing the player to adapt their strategy.

### 🚙 Normal Cars

Standard vehicles that follow traffic rules and wait for green lights.

### 🚌 Buses

Large vehicles that may move slower and occupy more road space.

### 😡 Impatient Drivers

Drivers who don't like waiting for too long and may create dangerous situations.

### 🚑 Ambulances

Emergency vehicles that need to cross the intersection as quickly as possible.

Ignoring them will heavily affect your performance report.

More vehicle types may be added in the future.

---

## 🚶 Pedestrians

Cars aren't your only responsibility.

Pedestrians also need to cross the intersection.

Keeping vehicle traffic moving while allowing pedestrians to cross safely creates another layer of decision-making.

Make them wait too long and your performance rating will suffer.

---

## 📊 End-of-Day Report

At the end of every day, your performance is evaluated.

The report can include statistics such as:

* Average vehicle waiting time
* Average pedestrian waiting time
* Number of vehicles successfully cleared
* Traffic flow efficiency
* Number of accidents
* Emergency vehicle response time
* Total traffic handled
* Overall performance rating

Example:

```text
DAY 4 REPORT

Vehicles Cleared:        482
Average Waiting Time:     21.4s
Pedestrian Waiting Time:  16.2s
Accidents:                 1
Ambulance Response:        8.7s

Performance: B

Officer Status: SAFE
```

---

## 👮 Don't Get Fired

Your performance matters.

A bad day won't necessarily end your career, but consistently poor traffic management will reduce your officer rating.

Too many:

* Accidents
* Traffic jams
* Long waiting times
* Delayed ambulances
* Unhappy pedestrians

…and the city may decide you're not the right person for the job.

### **Keep performing badly and you'll get fired.**

## 💡 Game Philosophy

The goal is for the game to have a clear difficulty curve:

> **Day 1:** "This is pretty relaxing."

> **Day 3:** "Okay, I actually need to pay attention."

> **Day 5:** "WHY ARE THERE SO MANY CARS?"

> **Day 7:** "WHO GAVE ME THIS JOB?!"

The player should slowly go from calmly controlling a small intersection to desperately trying to prevent an entire city block from collapsing into traffic chaos.
